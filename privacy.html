This policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you interact with the Vishaka Paark website and how that information may be used.

01 · INFORMATION 02 · COLLECTION 03 · HOW WE USE IT 04 · INFORMATION SHARING 05 · COOKIES 06 · SECURITY 07 · YOUR RIGHTS 08 · POLICY CHANGES 09 · CONTACT
01 · INFORMATION

Information We May Receive

When you contact Vishaka Paark or submit an enquiry through this website, you may choose to provide information such as your name, telephone number, email address and details relating to your stay.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably relevant to responding to your enquiry or providing requested services.

Your information belongs to you.

We do not ask for personal information unless it is reasonably necessary for handling your enquiry, reservation or communication.

02 · COLLECTION

How Information Is Collected

Information may be provided directly by you when you use website forms, contact the hotel by phone or email, or communicate with the hotel regarding a reservation.

Depending on the final website configuration, limited technical information may also be collected automatically by hosting, analytics or security services.

  • Information submitted through enquiry forms.
  • Contact information supplied during reservation enquiries.
  • Communication sent to the hotel's email address.
  • Basic technical information required for website operation and security.
03 · HOW WE USE IT

How We Use Your Information

Information provided by guests or prospective guests may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with reservations and communicate information relevant to a requested stay.

Information may also be used to improve website functionality, respond to support requests and maintain the security of the website.

  • Responding to booking enquiries.
  • Confirming or discussing room availability.
  • Communicating regarding a guest request.
  • Providing information requested by you.
  • Maintaining website security and reliability.
04 · INFORMATION SHARING

When Information May Be Shared

Personal information should be handled only for legitimate operational purposes connected with the requested service or enquiry.

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website, communicate with guests or process a requested service, subject to applicable requirements.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

We do not sell personal information.

Personal information supplied through this website is not intended to be sold as a commercial product.

05 · COOKIES

Cookies & Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the services and integrations enabled on the final website.

Cookies can help a website remember certain preferences, understand general usage and support functionality.

If third-party analytics, maps, booking services or other integrations are added, those services may have their own privacy policies and cookie practices.

06 · SECURITY

Protecting Information

Reasonable measures should be used to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting sensitive information through ordinary website forms unless specifically requested through a secure process.

07 · YOUR RIGHTS

Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information that has been provided to the hotel.

If you would like to ask about information associated with an enquiry, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern, please contact the hotel directly.

  • Ask what information was provided in an enquiry.
  • Request correction of inaccurate contact information.
  • Raise a concern about how information is being handled.
  • Ask questions about a specific communication or reservation enquiry.
08 · POLICY CHANGES

Updates to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the website, services, legal requirements or information-handling practices change.

Any updated version should be published on this page with an appropriate revised date.

Guests and website visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically if they continue to use the website.
